InstrumentRackTemplate.xml is adapted from the instrument_rack.adg.j2 template in
pdubs10/ableton-pack-generator (https://github.com/pdubs10/ableton-pack-generator),
licensed under WTFPL 2.0. The template's embedded plugin state and macro mappings
were removed; BubblePlug fills VST3 class IDs at export time.

AudioEffectRackTemplate.adg is Ableton Live's empty Audio Effect Rack preset,
used only as the device-group XML skeleton. BubblePlug replaces its chain devices
with VST3 device preset entries when exporting.
